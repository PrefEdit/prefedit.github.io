FAQ

Which apps does PrefEdit support?

All well-behaved Carbon and Cocoa apps. Classic Mac apps are not compatible due to their legacy storage system.

Is it safe to edit preferences?

Yes, with caution. Understand the values you're changing—incorrect edits can affect app or system stability. Always back up first.
